Frequently asked questions

Answers about products, orders, delivery and payment.

Are the listed products in stock?

Availability can change. Please submit an order request or contact us so we can confirm current stock before payment.

Are these prices final?

The starter catalogue contains illustrative TZS estimates, not verified live prices. Noah Digital Stores confirms the final price and exact configuration directly.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can submit an order request as a guest. The checkout asks for the contact and delivery details needed to follow up.

When should I pay?

Wait until Noah Digital Stores confirms the order and provides verified payment instructions. This website does not process payments.

Can I request a product that is not listed?

Yes. Send the product or specifications you need via WhatsApp or the enquiry form and we can check sourcing options.

How do I arrange delivery?

Provide your location in the order request. The business will confirm possible delivery or collection arrangements and any charge.

How can I track an order?

Use your order reference and phone number on the tracking page once the business order database integration is configured.
