Delivery and payment

We agree delivery and payment details with each customer before confirming an order.

Delivery

Delivery or collection options depend on the product, stock location and destination. Share your location in the checkout form and Noah Digital Stores will confirm available arrangements, timing and any delivery charge directly.

Payment

Choose your preferred payment arrangement in the order request. The business will confirm accepted payment methods and provide verified instructions after confirming your order. No payment is collected by this website.

Before paying

  • Confirm the exact product model and specifications.
  • Confirm the final total, including any delivery charge.
  • Verify payment instructions with Noah Digital Stores through the published contact details.
  • Keep your order reference and payment receipt.

The website does not process card payments or hold customer funds.

For sourcing services, customers pay the selected service fee before submitting the request. Noah verifies the payment reference before beginning the search. If you decide to purchase an item Noah finds, its price and delivery are agreed and paid separately using confirmed instructions. This website does not process payments.
