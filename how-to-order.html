How to order

A straightforward order request, without an account or sign-in.

1. Browse products

Explore the catalogue, search or filter by category. Each product page includes a description and available specifications.

2. Add items to your bag

Choose quantities in your shopping bag. Your bag stays available on this device when you move between pages or return later.

3. Submit an order request

Enter your name, phone, WhatsApp number, delivery preference, location and payment preference. You can order without creating an account.

Choose the sourcing service: TZS 1,000 to ask Noah to source a product, or TZS 2,000 to ask Noah to find the product and seller. Describe what you need, then pay using verified payment instructions from Noah Digital Stores and enter the transaction reference/code. Do not enter your payment PIN or OTP. The request cannot be submitted without the reference.

4. Wait for business confirmation

We will review your request and contact you to confirm stock, exact model, final price, delivery arrangements and payment instructions. Do not send payment until you have received and verified instructions directly from Noah Digital Stores.

After your request is recorded, Noah verifies the service-fee payment reference. Sourcing begins only after verification. We will then confirm available product options, product price and delivery with you; those costs are separate from the sourcing service fee.

5. Follow up

Your reference can be used to check status on the tracking page after the business has configured the order system.
